# Electrical and life-safety boundaries

This product plugs into household mains. The exposed PCB may have dangerous voltages, including after unplugging due to stored charge in capacitors.

- **Do not energize the device with the enclosure open.**
- Do not touch or probe exposed circuitry unless it has been rendered safe by someone qualified to assess the mains topology and capacitor discharge state.
- Do not connect USB adapters, logic analyzers, oscilloscopes, debuggers, or grounded equipment to the board on the assumption that its low-voltage circuitry is isolated.
- A transformer-looking component does not establish galvanic isolation or safe measurement reference points.
- No pad mapping, voltage measurement procedure, bypass, or hardware modification is provided here.
- Experimental outputs from a modified Ting are **not** a replacement for certified smoke/CO alarms, fire-risk monitoring services, or professional electrical inspection.

The repository documents observation and research, not a tested method for operating a modified mains-powered device.
